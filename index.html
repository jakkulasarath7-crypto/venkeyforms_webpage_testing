Our story

From Our Village to Your Home

At Venkey Farms, our story begins in the heart of our villages, where small farmers work hard every day to support their families and care for their cattle.

Many of these farmers cultivate their own land and raise cows and buffaloes alongside their farming activities. Some families have just three or four buffaloes, yet their dedication, hard work, and traditional way of life make them an important part of our journey.

We started Venkey Farms with a simple mission: to support small village farmers and bring quality products from our villages to your home.

Supporting Our Village Farmers

We collect fresh milk directly from small farmers in our villages and strive to offer them a fair price for their hard work. By building direct relationships with farmers, we aim to support their livelihoods and help them receive greater value for the milk they produce.

We believe every farmer deserves respect, every drop of milk deserves care, and every customer deserves quality they can trust.

Rooted in Nature, Made with Care

The farmers we work with often feed their cows and buffaloes with locally available green grass, dry fodder, and other traditional feed. We value these practices and the care they bring to milk production.

We carefully select the milk we collect and use it to prepare ghee through the traditional Bilona method: making curd, churning it into butter, and slowly heating the butter to obtain ghee. It is a process that connects our products to the heritage of Indian villages.

Our goal is to preserve traditional preparation methods and deliver products made with care, quality, and honesty.

A Bridge Between Villages and Cities

Venkey Farms is more than a brand. It is a connection between hardworking farmers in our villages and families living in our cities. By sourcing milk directly from small farmers and making products in our village, we aim to create rural opportunities and bring traditional village-made products closer to urban homes.

Every product represents our commitment to our farmers, our traditions, and the trust you place in us.

Our Promise

We believe good products begin with good intentions. That is why we focus on responsible sourcing, careful preparation, quality, and honest service. As we grow, our mission remains the same: to support small farmers, preserve traditional methods, and share village-made products with more families.
